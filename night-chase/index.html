🧟 Night Chase

Free zombie chase for your run. Zombies hunt your live GPS position on a dark map of your actual streets — keep moving and they fall behind, stop and they catch up.

How it works

  1. Grant location access, then start a run outdoors.
  2. In Survival, zombies spawn nearby and head straight for you. Tap a 💀 once it's close to shoot it (costs 1 ammo — collect 📦 crates as you run). The SHOVE button pushes back every nearby zombie at once, but costs a full battery bar, which charges as you cover distance.
  3. In Training, there's no horde — just a live pace readout against your target, for structured runs.
  4. Getting caught costs a life. Three strikes ends the run.

Before you start

Zombies move in a straight line toward your position — they don't pathfind along actual streets. The screen must stay on and this tab foregrounded the whole run (it requests a wake lock, but backgrounding the browser or locking the phone will pause tracking — a real browser limitation, not a bug). Works best held in hand, outdoors, with a clear GPS signal.

Free, no account, no ads, no server — everything runs in this tab. Not affiliated with any existing zombie-run app; this is an independent build in the same spirit. Run history is saved only in this browser's local storage on this device.
