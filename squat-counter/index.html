How to use it

  1. Tap Enable Motion Sensors (iPhone will ask permission).
  2. Put your phone in your pocket, waistband, or armband.
  3. Tap Start, stand still for a second, then squat away.
  4. Each rep gets a beep/vibration and a live count. If it misses a rep or over-counts, use −1 / +1 or adjust sensitivity above.

Detection is based on the magnitude of acceleration, so it works regardless of which way the phone is oriented in your pocket. It needs a real phone/tablet with a motion sensor — this won't work on a laptop.
